Privacy Policy

DushyStudio Pin Publisher · Last updated: November 17, 2026

DushyStudio Pin Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the DushyStudio Etsy shop (retro editable Canva templates). It publishes Pins of the shop's own original product designs to the shop owner's own Pinterest business account, each Pin linking back to its Etsy listing.

What data the app accesses

Using Pinterest's official OAuth authorization, the app accesses only the shop owner's own Pinterest account, with these permissions: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read and pins:write. It reads the owner's boards so Pins go to the right board, and creates Pins of the owner's own designs.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.
  • It does not collect passwords or login cookies. Access is granted only through Pinterest OAuth.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not save or repost other people's content.

Storage and security

The Pinterest access token is stored privately and used only to publish the owner's Pins. The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings under Security → Apps.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the DushyStudio shop on Etsy (etsy.com/shop/DushyStudio).
